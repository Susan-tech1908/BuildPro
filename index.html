Who We Are

Built on Trust,
Driven by Craft

BuildPro was founded in Nairobi in 2006 with a single mission: to deliver construction and design services with the quality and integrity that clients deserve. Over 18 years, we've grown into one of East Africa's most trusted multi-discipline firms.

Our team of over 80 skilled professionals — architects, engineers, interior designers, and master tradespeople — work in close collaboration on every project, no matter the size.

Licensed and fully insured across all service disciplines
10-year structural warranty on all new builds
Transparent fixed pricing — no hidden costs
Dedicated project manager for every client
Eco-friendly materials and sustainable building practices
